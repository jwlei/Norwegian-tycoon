Norwegian Tycoon card game rules

Norwegian Tycoon, known in Norwegian as Boms og President, is a President-style card game for 2–4 players. The aim is to get rid of all your cards first. The first player out becomes President, while the last player becomes the Bum (Boms).

This guide describes the rules used on playboms.eu. Other versions of President may use different house rules.

Card ranks and the first turn

Normal card ranks, from lowest to highest, are 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, jack, queen, king, ace and 2. The three of clubs is a special card.

The player holding the three of hearts starts the first round. In a two-player game, a random player starts instead. The Bum starts subsequent rounds.

Playing cards

On an empty table, you may play a single card, a pair, three of a kind or four of a kind. All cards in a set must have the same rank.

The next player must match the number of cards and play an equal or higher rank. For example, a pair of eights can be followed by another pair of eights, nines or higher, but not by a single card or three of a kind. The three of clubs has its own rules.

Passing and clearing the table

You may pass even if you have a legal move, including on an empty table. When everyone else passes on a pile, the table is cleared. The last player to play cards leads again. If that player has finished, the next active player leads.

The three of clubs and four of a kind

  • Three of clubs: Beats everything except four of a kind and clears the table. You lose if this is your last card.
  • Four of a kind played from your hand: Can be beaten by a higher four of a kind.
  • Four of a kind assembled on the table: Four matching cards built from singles or two pairs clear the pile and cannot be beaten.

Card exchanges and player counts

Your finishing position determines your role and the card exchanges before the next round. The three of clubs is excluded whenever a player must give away their best cards.

2 players

12 random cards are removed. The President receives the Bum's best card, excluding the three of clubs, and chooses one card to give back. The three non-club threes clear the table when collected at the top of the pile.

3 players

The three of diamonds is removed. The three of hearts and three of spades together at the top of the pile clear the table. The roles are President, Neutral and Bum.

The President receives the Bum's two best cards, excluding the three of clubs, and chooses two cards to give back.

The President may then force a one-card exchange with Neutral, followed by Neutral optionally forcing a one-card exchange with the Bum. Only the player initiating an exchange may skip it; the recipient cannot refuse. Both choose a card ranked from 7 through jack. Neutral cannot pass the card received from the President on to the Bum.

4 players

The roles are President, Vice President, Vice Bum and Bum. The President receives the Bum's two best cards, excluding the three of clubs, and chooses two cards to give back. The Vice President receives the Vice Bum's best card, excluding the three of clubs, and chooses one card to give back.

Turn timers and disconnections online

You have 120 seconds per turn, with one 120-second extension per round. A bot may take over after a timeout or disconnection. You can reclaim your seat when you return.

After each completed exchange, received cards are displayed for 3, 5 or 7 seconds according to your message-speed setting. Press Continue to become ready sooner. The next exchange or round starts when everyone is ready. Bots take 5–10 seconds for each exchange step.
