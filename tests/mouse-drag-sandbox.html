Mouse drag and flick sandbox

Uses the game's real hand, drag preview, flick detection and landing animations. Drag a card slowly onto the board, or flick upwards (10 px at 0.35 px/ms, within a 130-degree cone). On touch screens, start within the 130-degree upward cone to lift cards, or within 15 degrees of horizontal to scroll. Once lifted, cards can move freely and flick in the full cone. Mouse pickup remains immediate. A circle or slow pickup can finish with an upward flick: detection uses the last 80 ms of the final stroke, anywhere below the table. There is no total drag time limit; release within 120 ms of a recognized flick. Up to 3 px of lift-off recoil or a brief slowdown will not erase it; a deliberate reversal or sideways drag will. Small vertical jitter also stays in the same stroke before the flick first qualifies. Automatic hand centering uses the same easing over 180-700 ms, scaled to the scroll distance so small adjustments do not crawl through rounded pixel positions. Release slowly outside the board to return it. Click Reset to restore the hand.
