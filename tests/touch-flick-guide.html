Exact boundaries and timing
  • Initial pickup: 130 degrees, from -65 to +65 degrees from straight up, including the edges.
  • Scroll: within 15 degrees of horizontal. The 10-degree gaps between pickup and scrolling wait for clearer movement.
  • Initial intent needs 8 px on either axis. A 10 px diagonal at 45 degrees is therefore not enough to pick up yet. Scrolling needs 12 px horizontally.
  • After pickup: 130-degree release cone (-65 to +65). The mode stays locked: horizontal movement no longer scrolls.
  • Minimum flick: 10 px at 0.35 px/ms (350 CSS px/s). Straight up, that is 10 px in about 28.6 ms, or 28 px in 80 ms. The recent final stroke is sampled over 80 ms; movement before that can be slow.
  • Up to 3 px of vertical recoil does not split a short stroke, including before it first qualifies as a flick. Reversal is measured cumulatively from the furthest point, not separately per event.
  • A recognized flick stays valid for 120 ms through up to 3 px of lift-off recoil or a brief slowdown. A deliberate reversal or sideways drag cancels it; tiny movements do not restart that clock.
  • You can hold the card for seconds before finishing with a flick. A slow drop onto the table also works.
