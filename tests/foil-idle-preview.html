Subtle idle foil timing

Five timings for the same discreet effect: a small light drift and a gentle pulse, without moving the card. Each duration is one complete brighten-and-return cycle. Your approved foil colors and transparency are preserved.

Suggested starting point: 8 or 12 seconds. Four seconds makes the shimmer easier to notice; sixteen seconds is the most unobtrusive. Compare Normal (light) and Cosmic (dark) below.
