Foil transparency preview

Compare the unfoiled design and original untuned foil with 10-90% less foil opacity, in 10% steps. For example, 90% less opacity leaves only 10% of the original foil strength. The card artwork stays opaque. Move over any card to synchronize the light position.

Color, blend, printed-foil, and reflection controls affect only the reduced-opacity examples. The first two cards remain unchanged references. Active intensity applies to all foiled cards.

Ways to preserve the design

  • Suggested starting point: 50-70% less opacity, 35% saturation, soft-light blending, static printed foil off, and full neutral reflection on. This separates colored coverage from shine.
  • Neutral metallic: zero saturation with luminosity blending keeps the foil texture and light movement while largely preserving the card's hue. It can still change brightness and contrast.
  • Less persistent tint: disable static printed foil. It is a separate SVG layer beneath the CSS reflection, so reducing only the reflective layer would leave that tint behind.
  • Further production options: mask colored foil to borders or ornaments, protect ranks and illustrations, or show stronger color only during motion with a subtler resting effect. These are suggestions, not simulated by this preview.

This preview does not change the game's foil settings.
