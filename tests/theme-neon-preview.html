      Proposed UI changes

        Splash intro: on first entry the menu settles in, the sign's board fades up, the tubes power on with a short flicker, the glow follows and then the menu items rise in sequence (about 1.4 seconds). It animates only opacity and transforms, runs once per visit and is skipped with reduced motion.

        Neon sign: drawn as layered SVG on an acrylic backboard with standoff screws, tube supports, an unlit glass tube, a white-hot core, specular highlights, wall glow and a small transformer. Colours come from each theme's two neon colours. The glow layer pulses gently (opacity only, so it can stay on the compositor) and the lower line flickers briefly every few seconds. Reduced motion keeps the sign lit and still.
